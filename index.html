Who we are for

We don’t work with everyone. That’s on purpose.

The companies we work best with aren’t defined by size or stage, they’re defined by mindset. They believe in what they’re building, know they need help, and are ready to do something about it.

We love a hard problem. What we’re less suited for is the last-minute scramble: decisions already made, timeline fixed, just execute fast. We’re the team you bring in before that moment.

How we work

Three things that are always true.

You get us, not someone else

The people you talk to in the first conversation are the people who do the work. No handoffs, no account managers, no surprise juniors. Every client gets the actual team.

We stay small on purpose

We take a limited number of clients at a time. That’s not a capacity limitation — it’s how we make sure everyone gets our full attention. We’d rather do fewer things well.

We leave you more capable

Every engagement ends with your team knowing more than when we started. We explain our thinking, build systems you can use, and help you understand your own challenges.

We’re Brightside

Brightside is Trish and Brian, a designer and a marketer who started a small studio to stay close to the work and stay honest. Based in Long Beach, working everywhere. We believe the best work happens before the chaos starts, and the best thing we can do for any client is make sure they don’t need us forever.

Ready when you are

If your timing and ours line up, we’d love to hear what you’re working on. Drop us a line. We respond to every note. Usually pretty fast.
